Part (a) - Projection
I calculate the canvas aspect ratio in render() and use
orthographic(-aspect, aspect, -1, 1). This keeps the squares from
looking stretched when I resize the window.

Part (b) - Camera pan
I use cx and cy to move the camera with the arrow keys. I use -cx
and -cy because when the camera moves right, the world moves left.
This was confusing at first. It reminds me of cutting the back of
my hair using a mirror, where left and right can feel opposite.

Part (c) - Camera zoom
I use scaling(zoom, zoom) to zoom in and out and combine it with
translation(-cx, -cy) so the zoom stays around the camera center.
The + key zooms in, and the - key zooms out.

Part (d) - Second square
I added a second square using leftModel and rightModel. Each square has
its own position, but both use the same buffer, projection, and view.
I update the MVP matrix and draw each square separately.

Controls:
Arrow keys move the camera. + zooms in, and - zooms out.
