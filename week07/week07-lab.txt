Part (a): The canvas aspect ratio is calculated again in render(). The line
orthographic(-aspect, aspect, -1, 1) makes the visible world wider on a wide
canvas, so the square keeps its shape when the window is resized.

Part (d): I think moving the world instead of the camera is a little
confusing at first. Moving the world makes more sense when I think about
the view matrix. If the camera moves right, the world moves left. It
reminds me of cutting the back of my hair using a mirror. Left and right
can feel opposite, so I have to think the other way. In a game, it is
easier for me to think about the camera moving with the player. That is
why I used -cx and -cy so the arrow keys work the way I expect.

Controls: Arrow keys pan the camera. + zooms in, and - zooms out.
